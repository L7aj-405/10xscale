Questions

Asked on almost every call

Why can you guarantee results when no other agency does?

Because we only work with brands that pass the qualification above. When a store has traffic, customers, and an under-built email program, the revenue is already there — our job is recovery, not magic. That's why we can underwrite the risk instead of pricing it into a 12-month contract.

What if my flows are already set up?

Set up ≠ optimized. Most accounts we audit have flows that stop 1–2 emails too early, events that fire incorrectly, zero sunset policy, and no CLV segmentation. The audit will show you — and if your program is genuinely dialed in, we'll tell you that too, and you keep the report.

Do you also handle SMS?

Yes — from month 2–3 we integrate SMS inside Klaviyo with proper suppression logic, so you never double-message a customer.

Who writes and designs the emails?

We do. Strategy, copy, design, build, testing, reporting — done for you. You approve, we ship.

How fast until I see results?

Deliverability and tracking fixes typically show impact within days. Core flows compound over weeks 2–6. The guarantee is measured at day 90.
